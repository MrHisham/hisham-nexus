Founder

A multidisciplinary founder connecting healthcare, research, ethics, quality, and technology.

Dr. Hisham Essam Hasan is a clinical pharmacist, biomedical ethics researcher, and healthcare quality professional working at the intersection of clinical care, research integrity, regulatory documentation, and digital health innovation. His work is driven by a simple philosophy: technology should improve healthcare only when it is evidence-based, ethically governed, and designed around patient-centered outcomes.

Through Hisham Nexus, he translates complex scientific information into clear decisions, structured documentation, practical resources, and educational experiences for researchers, professionals, teams, and organizations.

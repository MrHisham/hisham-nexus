Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-06-08

1. Introduction

Hisham Nexus ("we", "our", or "the Brand") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard personal information when you visit this website, contact us, or request consulting, research, training, writing, quality, regulatory, digital adoption, or custom resource support.

2. Information We Collect

  • Contact information: name, email address, subject, and message details submitted through the contact form or email.
  • Project information: descriptions, goals, timelines, files, references, datasets, documents, or materials you voluntarily share for review or consulting.
  • Communication data: emails, meeting notes, inquiry details, feedback, and project-related correspondence.
  • Basic usage data: general technical information that may be processed by hosting, browser, or email services when you access the site or communicate with us.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To respond to inquiries and discuss potential projects.
  • To provide consulting, research support, writing, training, quality, regulatory, AI adoption, or custom resource services.
  • To prepare proposals, scopes of work, project plans, drafts, reports, training materials, or other agreed deliverables.
  • To communicate about timelines, revisions, payments, delivery, and follow-up support.
  • To comply with applicable legal, professional, ethical, or administrative obligations.

4. Data Protection & Confidentiality

Client information, project materials, research ideas, datasets, documents, and communications are treated with professional confidentiality. Where appropriate, Hisham Nexus can work under a Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) or written confidentiality terms before sensitive files are shared. We do not sell your personal information or client materials. Project files are retained only as needed for communication, delivery, legal, administrative, or agreed follow-up purposes unless a different retention arrangement is agreed in writing.

5. Third-Party Services

This website may rely on third-party services for hosting, fonts, email handling, analytics-free browser delivery, or communication. These providers may process limited technical information under their own privacy policies. The contact form currently opens your email application with a prepared inquiry draft instead of submitting information to a custom backend database.

6. Cookies

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. It may use local browser storage to remember your light or dark theme preference. Any essential cookies or technical storage used by hosting or browser services are limited to operating and displaying the site.

7. Your Rights

  • Request access to personal information you have shared.
  • Request correction or deletion of your personal information, where applicable.
  • Withdraw consent for future communication or processing where consent is the basis for processing.
  • Request a portable copy of information you have provided, where reasonably possible.

8. Contact Us
